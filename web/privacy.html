4. Lua engine (Fengari via jsDelivr)

To run Lua in your browser, the tool uses the open-source Lua interpreter Fengari, which is loaded from the content delivery network jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) when you open the page. jsDelivr is operated by Prospect One sp. z o.o., Kraków, Poland, and uses the networks of several providers, some of which are based in the USA. When the library is loaded, your IP address and technical request data are transmitted to jsDelivr. Your code is not transmitted.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in the reliable and fast delivery of the library the tool needs to work. For details, see the jsDelivr privacy policy.

5. Storage in your browser

So that your settings are still there the next time you open the tool, the following is saved in your browser's local storage:

  • deadeye.config: your current settings (preset, passes and tuning values)
  • deadeye.presets: the named presets you save yourself
  • deadeye.theme: whether you chose the light or dark theme

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings. The legal basis is § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, as storing these settings is necessary to provide the features you use.
